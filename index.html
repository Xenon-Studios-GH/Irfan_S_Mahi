About Me

Transforming Ideas Into Visual Stories

I'm Irfan Sadiq Mahi, a professional video editor and motion graphics artist with over 5 years of experience crafting visual narratives that captivate and inspire. Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, I've had the privilege of working with brands like TechBuddy, Xenon Studios, and MrNomster — each collaboration refining my storytelling instincts and pushing my creative boundaries.

From video editing and color grading to 3D animation and VFX, I bring a meticulous eye for detail to every project. I obsess over rhythm, pacing, and color psychology — the invisible threads that turn a sequence of clips into an unforgettable experience. Whether it's a cinematic brand film or a fast-paced social cut, my philosophy remains the same: precision, purpose, and passion.
