About Me

Transforming Ideas Into Visual Stories

I'm Irfan Sadiq Mahi, a professional video editor and motion graphics artist with over 5 years of experience crafting visual narratives that captivate and inspire. Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, I've had the privilege of working with brands like TechBuddy, Xenon Studios, and MrNomster.

From video editing and color grading to 3D animation and VFX, I bring a meticulous eye for detail to every project. My work spans music videos, documentaries, brand campaigns, and social media content — each piece tailored to tell a unique story.
