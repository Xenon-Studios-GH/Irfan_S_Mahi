### 👤 About Me

I'm **Irfan Sadiq Mahi** — a professional video editor and motion graphics artist with **over 5 years** of experience crafting visual narratives that captivate and inspire. Based in **Dhaka, Bangladesh**.

From video editing and color grading to 3D animation and VFX, I bring a meticulous eye for detail to every project. I obsess over rhythm, pacing, and color psychology — the invisible threads that turn a sequence of clips into an unforgettable experience.

🎯 **Philosophy:** Precision, purpose, and passion.
